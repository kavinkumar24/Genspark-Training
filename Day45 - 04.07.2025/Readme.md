## 2. Agile Methodology 
* Agile methodology is a project management framework that breaks projects down into several dynamic phases, commonly known as sprints. 
* It is a iterative methodology
### 4 pillers of agile
* Individuals over processes and tools  -> focus on team collabration rather than 
* Working software over comprehensive documentation 
* Customer collaboration - frequent acknowledge
* Responding to change - flexible and adapable

### 12 principles of agile

- Satisfy customers through early, continuous improvement and delivery
- Welcome changing requirements, even late in the project.  (don’t be afraid to take the changes or accept new ideas)
- Continuous delivery of value to your customers
- Encourage everyone to talk and work together across different areas of the project.
- Enthusiastic people, give them a clear mission (focused individuality trust)
- Face-to-face communication -> distributed team
- Delivering a working product instead of rely on documention
- Maintain sustainability throughout the development process.  (should not to be too fast)
- Continuous excellence enhances 
- Not overcomplicate things and find simple answers to complex problems. 
- Self-organizing teams generate the most value.
- Look back and reflect on their performance and adapt their behaviors for the future.


### Advantages

- adaptable
- collabrative
- focus on customer needs


### Type of Agile methodologies

- Kanban  (cards and columns like a draggable)
- Scrum (daily meetig to discus the active task and roadblocks)
